Support

MiraMake is a makeup practice app that compares your face with the face you want to become and shows how close you are as a number. All face analysis runs on your iPhone, and no account is required.

Frequently asked questions

Where are my photos stored?

Only on your iPhone. Face analysis runs on the device with a bundled model, and your photos are never sent anywhere. See the Privacy Policy for details.

How do I cancel MiraMake Plus?

On your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name at the top (Apple Account), then Subscriptions, then MiraMake Plus, then Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends and you will not be charged again.

I changed my iPhone. Can I keep the Plus I paid for?

Yes. Sign in with the same Apple Account on the new iPhone, open the Settings tab in the app, tap Plus, and then tap Restore purchases. There is no additional charge.

How do I delete my data?

Your photos, your ideal faces and your records live only on your iPhone. Deleting the app removes all of them. We keep no copy, so there is nothing to request from us.

Is the professional makeup review available?

Not in this version. That service is offered only in the Japan App Store. This app has no entry point for it, and no photo or analysis result leaves your device.
